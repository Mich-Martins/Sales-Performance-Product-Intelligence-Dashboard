# Business Problem

## Context

The project consolidates BMW sales records into a Power BI report for performance monitoring across time, models, geography, and sales channels. The source data includes revenue and quantity sold for 26 models in 24 countries from 2019 through 2023.

The dataset does not identify the commissioning organization, intended stakeholder, operational process, or whether the scenario is based on real business activity. 

## Problem Statement

Sales performance data distributed across dates, products, countries, regions, and channels is difficult to evaluate without a consistent analytical view. The dashboard is intended to help a stakeholder monitor revenue and sales volume, compare performance with the prior year, and explore the dimensions available in the dataset.

## Available Analytical Dimensions

* Date and year
* BMW model
* Region and country
* Sales channel
